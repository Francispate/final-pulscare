See how it works

Not sure what to click? Use the Help button

It is on every page, so you are never stuck.

  1. Look for the green Need help? button at the bottom left of the screen.
  2. Press it. A box opens with the steps for the page you are on.
  3. Read the steps in order. Press Show me next to a step and the page scrolls to the right button and highlights it.
  4. Still stuck? Use the email at the bottom of the Help box and tell us the page you were on.

In a medical emergency

Do not wait for an online doctor.

  1. Press the red SOS button at the top right of any page.
  2. Call 112 or the 108 ambulance straight from the box.
  3. Allow your location. We show the nearest hospitals and open Google Maps with directions.
